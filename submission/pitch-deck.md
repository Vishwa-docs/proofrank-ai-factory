# ProofRank Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1 - ProofRank

Bright Data-powered public AI product diligence.

Hackathon wedge: verify and rank AI Factory submissions with proof receipts.

## Slide 2 - Problem

AI product claims are easy to make and slow to verify.

Judges and sponsor teams must inspect public submissions, demos, repos, decks, and technology claims under severe time pressure.

## Slide 3 - Why Now

AI hackathons, accelerators, and enterprise innovation programs are producing more AI-native prototypes than reviewers can manually diligence. Public web evidence is available, but scattered and dynamic.

## Slide 4 - Product Workflow

Input event or project URL.

Run audit.

Extract claims.

Gather public evidence.

Run adversarial tribunal.

Run Originality Radar across the current field.

Separate the executed Bright Data proof bundle from planned, claimed, direct, pending, and failed evidence.

Score eligibility, Bright Data dependency, business value, originality, and presentation.

Export proof receipts and review actions.

## Slide 5 - Bright Data Architecture

Remote MCP: agentic live web access.

SERP API: prior-art and sponsor-claim discovery.

Web Scraper API and Web Unlocker: dynamic submission, demo, repo, and presentation evidence.

CLI: transparent reproducible commands for sponsor review.

## Slide 6 - Demo Results

ProofRank ranks the current AI Factory field, shows a Claim Ledger, Adversarial Tribunal, and Originality Radar for each project, highlights evidence gaps, and exports judge-ready receipts.

Key moment: Bright Data Dependency score separates executed sponsor-native evidence from superficial mentions.

## Slide 7 - Business Model And Ask

Customers: hackathon platforms, sponsor teams, accelerators, grants, procurement, enterprise innovation teams.

Model: per-event diligence workspace plus sponsor verification reports.

Ask: Bright Data prize and credits to expand live collection coverage across demos, GitHub, social, marketplaces, and product docs.
