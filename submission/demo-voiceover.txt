ProofRank is an evidence command center for AI hackathon review.

Start with public project links, then ProofRank ranks the field, checks the demo and repo, and separates claims from proof.

The Bright Data gate is load-bearing. scrape_as_markdown, search_engine, and discover must execute before the sponsor receipt passes.

Judges get a claim ledger, originality radar, adversarial notes, and a signed receipt with trace hashes.

The final packet exports as CSV, JSON, or Markdown. ProofRank turns a crowded submission page into a defensible winner shortlist.
