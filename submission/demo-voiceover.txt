ProofRank reviews public AI hackathon projects from their own links.

Start with public project links, then ProofRank ranks the field, checks the demo and repo, and separates claims from proof.

The Bright Data gate is load-bearing. scrape_as_markdown, search_engine, and discover must execute before the sponsor evidence passes.

Judges get a claim check, similarity check, review panel, and evidence receipt with trace hashes.

The final packet exports as CSV, JSON, or Markdown. ProofRank turns a crowded submission page into a defensible winner shortlist.
