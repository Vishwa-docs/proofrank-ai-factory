ProofRank reviews public AI hackathon projects from their own links.

Start with a GitHub repo and demo URL. ProofRank ranks the field, checks the demo and repo, and separates claims from evidence.

The Bright Data gate is load-bearing. Source scrape, search, and discovery must run before the evidence checks pass.

Judges get a verdict, shortlist, claim check, similarity check, review panel, and evidence report.

The final packet exports as CSV, JSON, or Markdown. ProofRank turns a crowded submission page into a defensible winner shortlist.
